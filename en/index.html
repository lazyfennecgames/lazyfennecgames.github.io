04 / ABOUT

The person behind Lazy Fennec.

I’m a computer engineer and mobile game developer. At a company I worked for remotely, I owned the games end to end as the sole developer: gameplay, UI design, ad integration and Google Play releases. On the web side, I work full stack, from front end to back end.

Today I publish my own games under the Lazy Fennec name and build fast, easy-to-manage websites for businesses.

Why Lazy Fennec?

I treat laziness as a method: instead of doing the same job ten times, I look for the shortest path that gets it right once. The fennec fox is one of the smallest yet smartest animals in the desert; it naps through the day and quietly gets things done at night. I’m a bit like that.
